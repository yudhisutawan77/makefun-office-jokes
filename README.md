## The joke

> HR says we're a family, so I asked who gets custody of us when the company sells us.

One line about office life, under 200 characters. It opens on the most
familiar corporate slogan — "we're a family" — then lands somewhere nobody
expects: custody hearings for employees. The laugh comes with the wince of
knowing the "family" gets sold off in an acquisition anyway.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUT/entry.txt`
when that variable is set). It validates the gate itself before writing:
UTF-8 text, 1 to 199 characters, a single line, not only whitespace.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
